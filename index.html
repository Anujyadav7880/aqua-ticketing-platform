Aqua Fun Water Park Info

About

Aqua Fun Water Park is a perfect destination for family and friends to enjoy thrilling water rides and relaxation.

Distance

Located 10 km from city center. Easy access by bus, car, and taxi.

Facilities

Changing rooms, lockers, food court, parking, and lifeguards available.

Things To Do

Water slides, wave pool, lazy river, kids play zone, and rain dance.

Warning

Follow safety rules. Children must be supervised. No running near pools.
